Cost shifting is not a fourth pricing model.

Dual pricing and surcharging change how a merchant presents costs to customers. They sit on top of the processing arrangement and require careful program design and compliance review.

Customer-Facing Approach

Dual Pricing / Cash Discount

The merchant displays a card price and offers a lower price to customers who pay with cash. The advertised and checkout experience must match the approved program design.

  • Focuses on the difference between cash and card prices
  • Requires clear, consistent disclosure
  • Implementation must match applicable rules and program requirements

Credit Cards Only

Surcharging

A surcharge adds a disclosed fee to eligible credit-card transactions. Debit and prepaid cards must not be surcharged, even when processed without a PIN.

  • Card-network registration and disclosures may apply
  • Network caps and state requirements are separate considerations
  • Never configure or quote a program from memory alone

Compliance Is Part of the Recommendation

Rules, caps, and state requirements can change. Confirm the approved program with Propelr Compliance or Legal before representing a specific structure, rate, or jurisdiction as permitted.
