We sort every merchant's hardware situation into one of three grades because it tells you, before you ever sit down, how much technical lift it's going to take to get them live, and that shapes your pitch, your timeline, and who else needs to be looped in. It also tends to track with the size of the business: Grade 1 is usually a small business with 1-2 locations and the owner directly involved in day-to-day operations. Grade 2 is more often a retail, grocery, or sit-down restaurant with more employees, inventory, and process to manage. Grade 3 is typically a franchise or multi-location operation where the POS decision isn't the owner's alone to make. Tap a card below for the full breakdown of each.
